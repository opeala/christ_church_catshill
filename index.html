Welcome to our Church

Our Victorian building provides an excellent setting for worship and is rich in local history, famously being the church where poet A.E. Housman was baptised as an infant.

Today, we have a strong mission-minded vision. We are always searching for new ways of reaching out and welcoming people. Whether you are exploring faith for the first time, looking for a new church family, or seeking a quiet place to reflect, you are warmly welcome here.

Vicar: Revd. Amanda Arthur

Leading our congregation since 2024, alongside a dedicated team of lay ministers who support our pastoral care, local school visits, and home communions.
