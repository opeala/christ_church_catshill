News & Notices

September 2026 updates for our congregation and community.

Building Update

Chancel Roof Repairs

Things are moving ahead, albeit slowly. We have authorised the builders to go ahead with the repairs to the roof, and are just awaiting a starting date from them. Likewise, we are waiting for the report from the Bat Society to see if we have bats roosting in the Church anywhere.

Next Steps: The architect, alongside representatives from the Diocese and Historic England, will be visiting on the 7th to survey the damage and determine the way forward.
Parish Event

Heritage Weekend & Volunteers Needed

Heritage Weekend will take place on the 19th and 20th of September, when the Church will be open all weekend. If anyone can spare a little time to come and "man" the Church, it would be greatly appreciated. All you have to do is meet, greet, and show people around if needed!

Also, if anyone can help put the bunting up outside Church, your help would be incredibly welcome.

Fundraising Concert

On Sunday afternoon (2:00pm – 4:00pm) before the "Singing Villeins" concert, tea, coffee, and cakes will be available. All donations will go towards the Chancel Roof repairs.

Funerals

Judy Haycock

Judy's funeral will take place on Tuesday 8th September at 1:15pm at Redditch Crematorium.

Marjorie Powell

Marjorie's funeral will take place on Monday 14th September at 10:15am at Redditch Crematorium, and then at 11:00am at Christ Church for the main service.

"The day will be as Marjorie wished it to be. Following the services, the wake will be held at The Holiday Inn on the Kidderminster Road."

Dress Code: Marjorie requested "no sombre clothing as she loved to be colourful and cheerful."

Flowers: Family flowers only. Memorial donations to chosen charities will be advised shortly.

Please let Vicky know if you will be attending the wake via Alan's email so the family have approximate numbers.
