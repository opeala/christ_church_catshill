Community & Friendship

Catshill Connect

Meet, chat, and reconnect with friends old and new. ALL are welcome!

A Space to Belong

In our fast-paced world, it's easy to feel disconnected. Catshill Connect was created to provide a warm, welcoming environment where members of our village can gather, share stories, and simply enjoy each other's company.

Whether you've lived in Catshill your entire life or have just moved to the area, you'll find a friendly face and a hot drink waiting for you. There is no agenda, no formal program—just a genuine opportunity to meet, chat, and reconnect with friends old and new.

Light Refreshments Included

What to Expect

  • Everyone is Welcome This group is open to everyone in the community, regardless of church background or belief.
  • Tea, Coffee & Cakes We provide a selection of hot drinks and tasty treats to enjoy while you chat.
